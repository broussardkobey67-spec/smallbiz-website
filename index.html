About us

A local crew that treats your yard like our own

Cedar & Stone started in 2010 with one truck and a simple idea: do honest, careful work and let the results speak. Today we're a small team of designers and growers who live and work right here in Wichita Falls.

We show up when we say we will, we clean up before we leave, and we stand behind every project with a one-year plant guarantee.
